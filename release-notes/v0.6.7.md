### Changed
- **Accounts** (Settings > Account) now use Fahh's own backend. New accounts confirm their email with a code, sent by StealthAI. Accounts made in v0.6.5 or v0.6.6 were test accounts on the old backend: create a new one.

Everything else is as in [v0.6.6](https://github.com/Arnav1771/fahh-ide/releases/tag/v0.6.6): git in the status bar, Outline, Timeline, Settings search, the account menu.
