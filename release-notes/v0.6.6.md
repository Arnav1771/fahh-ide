### New
- **Git in the status bar:** the current branch (`*` when there are changes; click opens Source Control) and **Sync Changes** with the commits to pull and push. A branch with no upstream gets **Publish Branch**. Pull and push use your installed `git` and its credentials, like a terminal does (#95).
- **Outline view:** the symbols in the active file, in any language (classes, functions, methods, structs, traits, top-level constants) and Markdown headings. The symbol at the cursor is highlighted; click to jump (#95).
- **Timeline view:** the commits that changed the active file, with author and age; click one to see what it changed (#95).
- **Settings search and navigation:** a search box filters every setting by name and description, with a section list to jump around (#96).
- **Account menu** in the activity bar: upload or download your settings, open account settings, or sign out; signed out, one click to sign in (#96).
- The terminal **+** button names the shell it starts, for example "New Terminal: PowerShell" (#96).
- Palette: Outline, Timeline, Sync Changes.
